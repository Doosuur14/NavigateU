# NavigateU — Online Assistant for International Students
NavigateU is an iOS app that helps international students (especially newcomers to Russia) navigate life, paperwork, and everyday needs. The app provides verified articles, step by step guides, and community shared experiences so students avoid common mistakes and integrate faster. 

🧭 Overview
A student first assistant: login, read and create experience based articles (documents, housing, speaking clubs, job opportunities), rate and save content  all organized to help foreign students settle in Russia safely and confidently.

🎯 Target Audience
International students in Russia (new arrivals and those with >2 years in country experience).
People relocating to Russia for study who need practical, local guidance.

🛠 Architecture & Tech Stack
iOS: Swift, UIKit (or SwiftUI if you prefer), MVVM architecture.
Backend / Storage: Firebase (Authentication + Firestore for articles and user data).
Other: Offline caching for saved articles (planned), simple rating algorithm (Firestore-based).

💡 Why NavigateU?
As a foreign student in Russia, I often struggled to find reliable information  from processing documents to understanding local systems.
NavigateU was born from that experience.
It’s more than an app; it’s a community tool built to ensure that other international students won’t have to go through the same confusion and frustration.
I’m continuously updating and refining the idea exploring new features, improving the design, and expanding its usefulness with every iteration.
